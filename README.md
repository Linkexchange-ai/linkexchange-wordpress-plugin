## What is LinkExchange?

**LinkExchange** is a WordPress plugin for website owners who want to improve their site's link health, strengthen internal linking, and find relevant backlink opportunities without leaving the WordPress dashboard.

It combines free SEO tools with access to a credit-based link placement marketplace. You can review potential placements, approve links, and use credits earned from verified placements to request backlinks for your own pages.

> **Free to get started:** Basic tools and a free plan are available. Certain marketplace functions, placements, or advanced features may have credit and plan limits; see the [current pricing](https://linkexchange.ai/pricing).

## Key Features

### 🔧 Broken Link Detection & Fixes

Find broken links and address link issues that can hurt usability. Review suggested fixes before updating your content.

### 📈 SEO Improvements

Improve your site's SEO foundations with actionable link-related recommendations. LinkExchange does **not** guarantee rankings or traffic increases.

### 🔗 Internal Linking

- Find orphan pages and pages that need more internal links.
- Discover relevant contextual internal linking opportunities.
- Help visitors and search engines navigate related content.
- Review linking recommendations from your WordPress dashboard.

### 🌐 Contextual Backlink Opportunities

Discover potential placements on participating sites based on topical and content relevance. Evaluate opportunities before committing credits or approving a placement.

### 🎯 Campaign Management

Set up campaigns for selected target URLs, review potential matches, and manage placement requests.

### ✅ Review & Approval

Keep editorial control over proposed link placements on your site. Review, accept, or decline requests before publishing.

### 💳 Credit-Based Marketplace

Earn platform credits from approved, successfully verified placements and use those credits toward eligible placements for your own campaigns.

### 🔍 Placement Verification

The platform verifies supported link placements as part of its credit and marketplace workflow.

### 📚 Content Indexing

Index eligible published content to help find relevant link opportunities.

## Installation

### Install from a GitHub Release

1. Visit the [latest release](https://github.com/Linkexchange-ai/linkexchange-wordpress-plugin/releases/latest).
2. Under **Assets**, download the **installable plugin ZIP** (not GitHub's automatically generated “Source code” archive).
3. Sign in to your WordPress admin dashboard.
4. Go to **Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin**.
5. Choose the downloaded ZIP file, then click **Install Now**.
6. Click **Activate Plugin**.

### Connect & Get Started

1. Open LinkExchange in your WordPress admin area.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions to connect or create your LinkExchange account.
3. Complete any required website verification or initial setup.
4. Allow eligible content to be indexed, where applicable.
5. Explore SEO/link-health tools, internal linking suggestions, and marketplace opportunities.

*Exact menu labels and setup steps can vary by plugin version.*

## How the Marketplace Works

1. **Connect your site** to the LinkExchange service.
2. **Index eligible content** so relevant placement opportunities can be identified.
3. **Review requests and opportunities** before accepting placements.
4. **Earn platform credits** for approved placements after successful verification.
5. **Use credits** to request eligible backlinks for your own campaigns.

Link opportunities depend on available participating sites and the matching criteria. A placement or ranking improvement is not guaranteed.

## Free and Paid Features

LinkExchange offers a free entry point, including basic internal linking features and a starter credit allocation for eligible new accounts. Advanced features and higher campaign limits may require a paid plan or additional credits.

See [plans and pricing](https://linkexchange.ai/pricing) for current limits and details.

## Privacy & Data Handling

Connecting a website to the LinkExchange service can involve sharing website information, eligible published content and metadata, links, and campaign details for indexing, matching, or verification.

Before connecting a production website, read the applicable [Privacy Policy](https://linkexchange.ai/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://linkexchange.ai/terms). Avoid sharing private customer information or credentials.

## Responsible SEO & Link Management

Choose placements for genuine editorial relevance and reader value. Follow applicable search-engine policies, including appropriate link attributes such as `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"` when required.

**LinkExchange does not guarantee backlinks on specific sites, search rankings, traffic, or revenue.**
